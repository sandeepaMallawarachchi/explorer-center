The Hambantota District stretches along Sri Lanka’s southeastern coast, bordering four other districts. The district has a very dry climate, and salt production is an economic activity that happens mostly in the district’s capital of the same name.

The capital of Hambantota was severely damaged by the Indian Ocean Tsunami in 2004, and major reconstruction efforts have been underway ever since.

*Yala National Park*
![Yala National Park](img/BuddhangalaMonastery.jpg)

Yala national park is undeniably the top most visited highlight based in Hambantota and so as in southern Sri Lanka. Known as the second largest national park in the country, Yala is surely the most popular and also the place where you will find a great variety of wildlife at a single place.

The atmosphere of the national park is very inspiring and appealing. Even though you can see almost all of your favorite wild animals most get attracted here to explore exotic leopards. Also you can see a great collection of flora and fauna in a dry zone setting.

*Hummanaya*
![Hummanay](img/BuddhangalaMonastery.jpg)

Hummanaya is a natural blowhole and is caused when sea water rushes through a submerged cavern and is pushed upwards. The sea water flows underneath the shore and then comes out of this hole due to pressure. The water fountain created by the geological feature shoots up every couple of minutes, depending on the nature of the sea, with the spray often reaching as high as 25 m (82 ft) to 30 m (98 ft).

*Ridiyagama Safari Park*
![Ridiyagama Safari Park](img/BuddhangalaMonastery.jpg)

Southern island is a great set up for wild highlights . You will come across many different national park settings in Hambantota district alone and one Ridiyagama national park is also a great place to revel the best of nature in a dry zone setting.

Most travelers who visit Yala national park keep some space to explore Ridiyagama national park as well in order to get a better exposure about nature. The national park is known as first ever man made national park in Sri Lanka.

The park is mostly celebrated for mammals and bird species and is spread over 500 hectares. If you love less crowded, moderate, serene national park setups surely adding Ridiyagama National Park will offer you an unforgettable experience for your stay.

*Bundala National Park*
![Bundala National Park](img/BuddhangalaMonastery.jpg)

From the series of national parks available in Sri Lanka Bundala national park gets a special attention as it is recognized as a mesmeric wonderland that is full of fascinating natural sceneries. At this amazing national park you will encounter thousands of multi hue birds with a wide range of species.

Bundala is a paradise that produces a great showcase of lagoons, waterways, and dunes. If you feel like get connected to nature for awhile from your mind and body while you stay in mesmerizing Hambantota all you may need sometimes is a good time to spend in this tranquil and scenic national park.

Compared to many other national parks in Sri Lanka, Bundala still portrays a complete natural set up of a natural park while most others are distributed by crowdsourcing settings.

Homing about 200 species of birds, Bundala is also setting a fine atmosphere to some other wild animals as well. If you are fond of wildlife and a person who love and adore nature probably Bundala would be a good choice for you to add to your travel diaries.


*Mirijjawila Botanical Garden*
![Mirijjawila Botanical Garden](img/BuddhangalaMonastery.jpg)

Mirijjawila Botanical Garden is another one of the most visited sites based in southern Sri Lanka attractions. Sri Lanka offers different range of botanical gardens at different locations, and the specialty about Mirijjawila Botanical Garden is it has identified as a dry zone botanic garden in Sri Lanka.

The botanical garden is located in a place name Mirijjavila which makes the garden called as Mirijjawila Botanical Garden. The garden is opened very recently as of the year of 2013 and is one of the five dedicated botanical garden in Sri Lanka.

You can access Mirijjawila Botanical Garden either from Colombo- Katharagama main road and the other from Beragama road which is connected to Sooriyawewa-Colombo road. The botanic garden is opened for public throughout the year and usually you will find it crowded most of the time with local and foreign tourists.

The purpose of establishing the garden is with a concept of preserving and presenting the bio diversity of the country and so as you will get inspired findings an incredible amount of plants, shrubs and trees. It’s a complete natural backdrop that makes it picturesque and attracts more visitors who love pictures.

*Walawe River Safari*
![Walawe River Safari](img/BuddhangalaMonastery.jpg)

Add some diversity to your travel diaries with a distinguish experience. A sail in the famous river Walawe on a fine evening or dawn will surely will be an unfortunate memory that you can treasure in lifetime.

The exciting excursion of exploring the waterbody in Walawe river in details would be an exciting journey for you. Walawe river safari is slightly different to what you usually get to see in Sri Lanka as the journey is facilitated by local community group.

The experience offers by this program has recognized in its next level where it has rewarded as best community based tourism project in Sri Lanka. Hence if you are touring in Hambantota don’t forget to add this unique experience to your itinerary.

Walawe river safari is one of the best ways to identify the best natural wonders habitat based in Walawe river whereas you will be amazed by the great variety of flora and fauna that you will discover alongside. The river safari will lead you to beautiful beach spots as well where you can take nice pictures and refresh yourself with a fresh king coconut.

*Bata Atha Farm*
![Bata Atha Farm](img/BuddhangalaMonastery.jpg)

Agro Technology Park in Bata Atha (Bata Atha Farm) is located in the Hungama Batatha area in the Hambantota District in the Southern Province, this is another wonderful creation that has attracted local and foreign tourists. It is a kind of farm owned by the Department of Agriculture. But this is an open place for tourists from outside. There is no charge to enter here. You can also get a good clean "Ranawara" throat drink with no artificial ingredients and Things like vegetables and fruits can be bought for money.

Inside the garden, there is an orchard, a vegetable garden as well as a medicinal garden, but don't go picking nuts from them.

This place is very famous among the lovers of the area. The most amazing thing is that the lovers who come here and fall in love with each other come here at the moment of their wedding and to take their wedding photos. Then one day a child can come with a bag.
