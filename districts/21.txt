*Kurunegala Clock Tower*
![Kurunegala Clock Tower](img/kurunegalaclocktower.webp)

The Kurunegala Clock Tower, one of the top attractions in the city, is a famous landmark with historic importance. Originally built in 1922 in memory of soldiers’ lives sacrificed in World War I, this landmark stands tall in the present day. This acclaimed monument made out of granite stone, cement, and wood is found right in the center of the city.

From here, you can also go to Kurunegala Court of Law, the Central Market, and the Central Bus Stand which are in the area.

*Ethkanda Raja Maha Viharaya*
![Ethkanda Raja Maha Viharaya](img/EthkandaRajaMahaViharaya.webp)

Also known as Ethkanda Viharaya, this attraction is a sacred Buddhist temple. The exact history of this temple is unknown, however, people believe that this temple was built around the Dambadeniya era, a time when monarchs ruled during the 14th to 15th centuries.

You will find two caves in the temple’s capacity. The first temple is the bigger of the two and contains the main Buddha shrine. Several artworks not just from the modern times but also those inspired by the Kandyan tradition are currently housed here.

*Welagedara Stadium*
![Welagedara Stadium](img/WelagedaraStadium.webp)

If you’re a fan of cricket, don’t miss out on going to Welagedara Stadium. This spacious stadium is known for being the venue of many local and international games, as well as other types of events. You can even see Athugala Viharaya from here, which makes it a great spot for taking pictures.

At least 10,000 people can watch a game in this stadium, so don’t hesitate to bring along your friends with you. Many important games were played in this stadium, like the 2008 Women’s Asia Cup and the 2000 Under-19 Cricket World Cup.

*Kurunegala Lake*
![Kurunegala Lake](img/KurunegalaLake.webp)

This lake is found right in the center of the city, featuring lush greenery and wonderful sunset views. The area is so relaxing that you might even see a couple being wedded here from time to time. Feel free to bring along your bike and cycle down the 3.5-kilometer (2.1-mile) track for some light exercise.

The water looks gorgeous at nighttime, so don’t hesitate to go for an evening walk too. A handful of dining places can be found nearby. This lake is a useful part of the city, since it’s essential for the town’s agriculture.
