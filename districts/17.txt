*Nilaveli Beach*
![Nilaveli Beach](img/NilaveliBeach.jpg)

Nilaveli Beach is often recognized as one of the most Trincomalee-visit places in Sri Lanka. This strip of beach is located roughly 16 kilometers north of Trincomalee.

It's the starting point for Pigeon Island but also ideal for watersports, relaxation, and sunbathing. The smooth white sand and brilliant blue waves make this beach extraordinarily scenic and appealing. The sea is warm, shallow, and safe to swim in, unlike many of Sri Lanka's beaches with strong currents.

This area is home to several large hotel resorts, making it an ideal location for a relaxed vacation.

*Koneswaram Temple*
![Koneswaram Temple](img/KoneswaramTemple.jpg)

The Koneswaram Temple is an old Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Shiva. It is Trincomalee's most famous temple, with a breathtaking clifftop position. This should undoubtedly be on your list of Trincomalee attractions!

Sri Lankan temples are usually smaller than those in other parts of Asia, notably Thailand and India. However, Koneswaram Temple is one of the greatest sites to visit in Trincomalee due to its beauty and uniqueness.

It's an old temple to the Hindu deity Shiva, surrounded by breathtaking surroundings. If you only visit a few temples while exploring Sri Lanka, make this one of them.

It's also known as the Temple of 1000 Pillars, and the exquisite detail only adds to its splendor. If you arrive early, you may enjoy a stunning dawn.

*Snorkelling At Pigeon Island National Park*
![Snorkelling At Pigeon Island National Park](img/PigeonIslandNationalPark.jpg)

Unquestionably one of the best places to visit near Trincomalee, the pure and scenic Pigeon Island National Park off the coast of Nilaveli provides an excellent escape from the city.

The national park, which consists of two tiny islands, is home to endangered rock pigeons, diverse marine life, and breathtaking rock formations. The pristine white sands, dazzling rock pools, and shallow blue seas make for an unforgettable excursion, as do the numerous snorkeling chances just off the beach.

You must take a boat to the island, which takes around 20 minutes, and pay an admission fee to access Pigeon Island National Park. All the beaches offer day tours to Pigeon Island, so if you want to go on an arranged tour, simply choose one.

You will get to see the sea life that lives in the shallow seas around Nilaveli. Admire the gorgeous corals and glittering fish. If you have never snorkeled before or simply enjoy snorkeling, this is one of the best spots to visit in Trincomalee.

*Whale watching near Swami Rock*
![Whale watching near Swami Rock](img/WhalewatchingnearSwamiRock.jpg)

Trincomalee is famed for whale viewing. It has the opposite season as the South Coast of Mirissa, so if you missed it there, you may catch it here.

Swami Rock is a well-known monument located near the Koneswaram Temple. The rock at the summit of a holy cliff provides stunning panoramic views of Trincomalee.

Swami Rock is also an excellent area to watch whales from the shore, especially between June and September. During these months, a significant number of whales (including the rare blue whale) pass by the shore of Trincomalee.

This superb vantage position lets you view far out to sea, making it a fantastic (free) alternative to a whale-watching boat tour. Dolphins are likely to be seen alongside whales.
