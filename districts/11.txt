13. Kilinochchi

2021 Population Estimate: 133,000
Total Land Area: 1,205 km2 (465 mi2)
Province: Northern

The Kilinochchi District is the second northernmost district in Sri Lanka, just under the district of Jaffna. It was part of the region of Rajarata until the 13th Century AD, then became part of the Kingdom of Jaffna before coming under British, Portuguese, and British control. The current borders of this district were established in 1984.

The capital of Kilinochchi was the capital of the Tamil Tigers during the Sri Lankan Civil War. The city was created in 1936 as part of an effort to ease overpopulation in nearby Jaffna. Most of the population in this area is involved in agricultural work.

*Kilinochchi Tank*
![Kilinochchi Tank](img/BuddhangalaMonastery.jpg)

This tank is located in the heart of the city and is a great spot for a peaceful stroll. The tank is surrounded by lush green foliage and is a great spot for bird watching. It is also a great place to take some stunning photos of the city.

*Elephant Pass War Memorial*
![Elephant Pass War Memorial](img/BuddhangalaMonastery.jpg)

Elephant Pass War Memorial is situated at Jaffna peninsula, right after you pass the Elephant pass saltern on A9 road. It has been constructed to commemorate late Corporal Gamini Kularathne, who lost his life during his effort to safeguard the Elephant Pass camp in 1991.

*Hasalaka Gamini War Memorial*
![Hasalaka Gamini War Memorial](img/BuddhangalaMonastery.jpg)

a memorial to Corporal Gamini was later set up in Elephant Pass with the remains of the bulldozer he destroyed. The memorial, as well as the destroyed vehicle, can be seen on the A9 road on the way to Jaffna.

*Baobab Tree Vallaipadu*

This tree is not visible to main roads. It is in the jungle. When you follow the Google map, very close to the location of the tree, a white cross is erected besides the cart road. Near this cross, a small path is there into the jungle. This path leads to baobab tree.
