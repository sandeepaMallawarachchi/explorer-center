Monaragala is the second-largest district in Sri Lanka. It lies in the southeast corner of the country, bordering four other districts, and has no coastline. The capital of the district is also its largest city, and it’s located just southeast of Badulla, the capital of the Uva Province.

The district’s economy is largely based on agriculture, and the capital lies approximately 170 kilometers east of Colombo.

This ancient Buddhist temple is believed to be the earliest Buddhist temple in Sri Lanka, and dates back to the 3rd century BC. The temple is a popular pilgrimage site and is known for its magnificent architecture and artwork.

*Kurundu Oya National Park*
![Kurundu Oya National Park](img/BuddhangalaMonastery.jpg)

This national park is located in the eastern part of the district and is home to a variety of wildlife, including elephants, leopards, wild boar, and a variety of bird species.

This ancient Buddhist monastery is located in the village of Ritigala and dates back to the 3rd century BC. The monastery is known for its beautiful architecture and artwork and is a popular pilgrimage site.
