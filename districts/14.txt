*Mullaitivu Beach*
![Mullaitivu Beach](img/beach.webp)


The beach at Mullaittivu is long, white and beautiful. It's undeveloped, although there is some construction in the town, as stores are being built. There is a simple cafe run by a military officer back behind the sand. The surf ranges from mild to treacherous.

*Nayaru Lagoon*
![Nayaru Lagoon](img/NayaruLagoon.webp)

It's a quite natural site for a photo shoot . Hard rock belt on coast of sea make some wonderful scenes . Though it beautiful ,it's much dangerous too. Please take extra caution while walking or taking photos in shore or around river side . Sea is mostly rough here better avoid swimming .
