Kandasamy Kovil Temple
![alt text](img/KandasamyKovilTemple.jpg)

Kandasamy Kovil in Vavuniya is not only a renowned Hindu temple but also a remarkable tourist destination, a true Vavuniya gift, perfect for exploring with your loved ones. Adorned with a gold-clad figure and an intricately designed entryway tower, this stunning temple exudes magnificence from its sacred site. Delving deeper into its premises reveals a hint of mystery, adding to its allure.

Dedicated to the deity Murugan, the temple draws large crowds of worshippers, particularly during festivals, making it a bustling hub of activity. The temple's marriage hall also hosts traditional wedding ceremonies, adding cultural richness to its offerings. For those intrigued by diverse religious rituals, visiting Kandasamy Kovil promises a captivating and enriching experience.

Jethawanaramaya Stupa
![alt text](img/jethawanaramasthupa.jpg)

At 400 feet tall, Jethawanaramaya Stupa is the highest stupa in the world and is part of the UNESCO World Heritage Site of Anuradhapura. Though the Stupa may no longer be the tallest building due to the effects of time, it is still an incredible construction work.

The stupa is a stunning example of the incredible technical skills employed in the monument's creation. It is the most significant building in the world, constructed primarily of bricks. It is thought that King Mahasena laid the groundwork for Mahinda's ultimate burial place when he constructed the Jethawanaramaya Stupa.

Wilpattu National Park
![alt text](img/WilpattuNationalPark.jpg)

Famous as the ultimate destination in Sri Lanka for seeing and exploring leopards, Wilpattu Park is one of the largest and most diverse national parks in the country.

Explore Wilpattu Park for your fill of captivating wildlife experiences at this national treasure. The abundance of natural lakes adds to the unique appeal, while the opportunity to be among the wilderness in park camps enhances the adventure. Just an 80-minute drive from central Vavuniya, this park promises an unforgettable journey into the wild.

Isinbassagala
![alt text](img/Isinbassagala.jpg)

Isinbassagala is a remarkable tourist destination located around 21.4 kilometers from Vavuniya's center. Situated on a hill, the enormous Stupa and Buddhist temple offers sweeping views of the surrounding rolling hills. Enormous statues of King Devanampiyatissa and the Buddha are visible alongside the Bodhi tree. This temple was built on King Devanampiyatissa's orders, and in the 20th century, it received restorations and repairs.

Along with the remnants of the temple, excavations at this site also uncovered a stone etched with the image of a serpent (Naga Gala) and the footprint of Buddha. These findings show the historical periods of Sri Lanka. Before the teachings of the Buddha were widely practiced, the supreme deities were nagas or cobras.
