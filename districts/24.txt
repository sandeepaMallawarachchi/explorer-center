*Demodara Nine Arch Bridge*
![DemodaraNineArchBridge](img/DemodaraNineArchBridge.webp)
The Demodara Nine Arch Bridge is a marvel of engineering and a testament to Sri Lanka’s colonial heritage. This picturesque bridge, constructed during the British colonial era, is located amidst breathtaking tea plantations.

Visitors can witness a train passing over the bridge, creating a stunning sight. Nine Arch Bridge is a famous tourist destination among the best places to visit in Badulla.

*Adisham Bungalow*
![AdishamBungalow](img/AdishamBungalow.webp)
Adisham Bungalow, also known as Adisham Monastery, is a charming colonial-era mansion turned monastery. Surrounded by well-maintained gardens and picturesque landscapes, this tranquil retreat offers visitors a glimpse into the lives of the Benedictine monks who reside here.

*Ravana Falls*
![RavanaFalls](img/RavanaFalls.webp)
Ravana Falls is a picturesque waterfall believed to be associated with the ancient Indian epic, Ramayana. Legend has it that King Ravana’s wife used the pool formed by the falls for bathing. Surrounded by mist-covered mountains, this waterfall provides a serene setting for relaxation and rejuvenation.

*Dhowa Rock Temple*
![DhowaRockTemple](img/DhowaRockTemple.webp)
The Dhowa Rock Temple is an ancient Buddhist temple nestled amidst lush greenery. It is renowned for its magnificent rock-cut Buddha statue, which dates back to the 1st century BC. The serene ambiance and intricate cave paintings make this temple a must-visit attraction in Badulla.

*Muthiyangana Raja Maha Viharaya*
![MuthiyanganaRajaMahaViharaya](img/MuthiyanganaRajaMahaVihara.webp)
Muthiyangana Raja Maha Viharaya is a sacred Buddhist temple with deep historical significance. It is believed to have been visited by Lord Buddha during his third visit in Sri Lanka. The temple’s architectural beauty and tranquil surroundings make it a popular pilgrimage site.

*Dunhinda Falls*
![DunhindaFalls](img/DunhindaFalls.webp)
Dunhinda Falls is a mesmerizing waterfall cascading down from a height of approximately 64 meters. Surrounded by dense greenery, the falls offer a breathtaking sight and a perfect spot for nature enthusiasts and photographers alike.

*Uva Halpewatte Tea Factory*
![UvaHalpewatteTeaFactory](img/UvaHalpewatteTeaFactory.webp)
For tea lovers, a visit to the Uva Halpewatte Tea Factory is a must. This renowned tea factory provides an insight into the tea-making process, from plucking the leaves to the final product. Visitors can also enjoy a refreshing cup of Ceylon tea while savoring the panoramic views of the surrounding tea plantations.

*Namunukula Mountain Range*
![NamunukulaMountainRange](img/NamunukulaMountainRange.webp)
The Namunukula Mountain Range is a paradise for hikers and nature enthusiasts. With its mist-covered peaks, lush forests, and breathtaking viewpoints, it offers a rejuvenating escape from the hustle and bustle of city life. Exploring the trails and witnessing the sunrise from the mountaintops is an unforgettable experience.

*Bogoda Wooden Bridge*
![BogodaWoodenBridge](img/BogodaWoodenBridge.webp)
The Bogoda Wooden Bridge is a remarkable architectural marvel that dates back to the Kandyan period of Sri Lanka’s history. Constructed entirely with wood and held together by intricate wooden pegs, this bridge offers a glimpse into the country’s rich heritage and engineering prowess.

*Diyaluma Falls*
![DiyalumaFalls](img/DiyalumaFalls.webp)
Diyaluma Falls is the second highest waterfall in Sri Lanka, cascading down from a height of approximately 220 meters. The stunning natural beauty of the falls, surrounded by dense forests and mist, creates a captivating spectacle. Visitors can also swim in the natural pools formed by the cascading water.

*St Mark’s Church*
![StMarksChurch](img/StMarkChurch.webp)
The St. Mark’s Church is an Anglican church situated in the central ward of the Badulla Municipal Council in Sri Lanka. It is a must-visit destination for tourists looking to spend a peaceful day with their family and friends.

During the colonial era, the British rulers developed Badulla into a remarkable location, complete with horse riding facilities, golf grounds, and tennis courts. The church is surrounded by breathtaking greenery, creating a serene and picturesque atmosphere that adds to its allure.

*Old Welekade Market*
![OldWelekadeMarket](img/OldWelekadeMarket.webp)
The famous Old Welekade Market in Badulla is a must-visit for shoppers in search of souvenirs during their trip to Sri Lanka.

This market not only offers a wide range of local handcrafted items but also transports travelers back to a bygone era. The market is housed in a historic colonial building constructed by the British in 1889, which exudes a remarkable charm.

The pavements of the Old Welekade Market, set within this architectural gem, captivate visitors with their allure. This well-preserved building showcases the grandeur of British architecture and holds the status of an Archaeologically Protected Monument.

Local residents also frequent this market, spending quality time within its premises. Situated on the escarpment near the paddy fields, the name “Welekade” itself translates to “the shop of the paddy fields,” perfectly reflecting its location
